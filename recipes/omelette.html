Omelette

A very simple dish that you can cook easily and enjoy it anytime. With just egg and salt you can make it easily and perfect with a hot bowl of rice.

Ingredients

  • 2/3 eggs
  • slices onions
  • slices chilli(if you prefer spicy)
  • oil
  • salt

Steps

  1. Season the beaten egg with salt and peppers.
  2. Pour the eggs into the pan and wait for it to spread the pan.
  3. Add sliced onions and chilli and scatter over the top of the omelette.
  4. Fold it gently and put in the plate. Now serve it with a hot bowl of rice.
